1. The app

Kipi Kids offers reading (phonics in English), drawing, coloring, coding, vocabulary, math, logic, music and game activities for children aged 3–10. In the free version, drawing, coloring, music and games are unlimited. Everything is open for the first 3 days; until the end of the first week, the whole of Today's Lesson and 5 different activities a day in the learning sections are free, and after that a 3-task Today's Lesson and 3 different activities a day. The days are counted from the day the app is first used on the device, also after it is reinstalled. The reading section is available when the app language is English or Turkish.
